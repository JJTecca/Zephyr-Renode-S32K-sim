# fail-operational vs fail-safe

Fail-safe = stop safely · fail-operational = keep working · fail-degraded = keep working, reduced.
The paragraph that disarms the hardest defense question states which one each ACT action implements:
[[service restart]] = fail-operational **attempt** · [[degraded mode]] = fail-degraded ·
load-shed = fail-safe. See [[ISO 26262]], [[Safety Cage Pattern]] and the supervisor whitelist.
