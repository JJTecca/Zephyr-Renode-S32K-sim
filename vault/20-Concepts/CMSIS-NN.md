# CMSIS-NN

Arm's optimized int8 neural-network kernels for Cortex-M. With **no NPU on the [[S32K3]]**, this
is what buys inference speed on the M7 — and comparing with/without CMSIS-NN is a free result
for the latency table in [[Metrics]]. Used by [[LiteRT for Microcontrollers]] under the DETECT stage.
