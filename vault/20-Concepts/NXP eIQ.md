# NXP eIQ

NXP's model optimization/deployment toolchain for S32; wraps [[quantization]] and runtime integration — its MCU engine today is TFLite Micro / [[LiteRT for Microcontrollers]]. Also: **eIQ Time Series Studio**, NXP's autoML for on-MCU time-series anomaly detection — not a component of ours, but a free baseline to beat ([[ADR-012 — Renode is the plan of record]]).
