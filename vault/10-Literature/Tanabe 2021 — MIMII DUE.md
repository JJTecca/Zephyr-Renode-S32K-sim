# MIMII DUE: Sound Dataset with Domain Shifts due to Changes in Operational and Environmental Conditions

## Summary (my words to verify on first read)
Extends MIMII with domain shifts: feature distributions change between training and operation due to operating and environmental conditions.

## Relevance to my thesis
Domain shift is exactly what a vehicle does (idle vs highway); informs how we normalize [[telemetry]] per operating mode before DETECT.
