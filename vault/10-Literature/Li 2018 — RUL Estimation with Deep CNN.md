# Remaining Useful Life Estimation in Prognostics Using Deep Convolution Neural Networks

## Summary (my words to verify on first read)
Applies 1-D CNNs over sliding sensor windows on C-MAPSS, beating classical RUL regressors without hand-crafted features.

## Relevance to my thesis
Shows window-CNNs are enough for degradation trends — relevant because a tiny CNN quantizes better than an LSTM for possible on-K3 [[RUL]] hints.
