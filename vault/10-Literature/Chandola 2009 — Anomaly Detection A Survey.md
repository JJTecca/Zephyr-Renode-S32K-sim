# Anomaly Detection: A Survey

## Summary (my words to verify on first read)
The canonical taxonomy of anomaly detection: point/contextual/collective anomalies, and technique families (statistical, distance, density, spectral). Still the cleanest vocabulary for framing what an anomaly *is* in a given domain.

## Relevance to my thesis
Gives the thesis its definitions chapter: our injected faults are mostly **contextual** ([[thermal drift]] is only anomalous relative to load) and **collective** ([[CAN error frames]] bursts). Anchors [[anomaly detection]].
