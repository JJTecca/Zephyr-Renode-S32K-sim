# LSTM-based Encoder-Decoder for Multi-sensor Anomaly Detection

## Summary (my words to verify on first read)
Trains an [[LSTM]] encoder-decoder to reconstruct normal multi-sensor sequences; reconstruction error flags anomalies on engine and machine data.

## Relevance to my thesis
The sequence-model alternative to our window [[autoencoder]]; we benchmark against this idea and cite it when arguing windows-vs-sequences for MCU budgets.
