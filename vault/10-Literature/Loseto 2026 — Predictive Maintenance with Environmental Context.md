# AI-Driven Predictive Maintenance with Environmental Context Integration for Connected Vehicles

## Summary (my words to verify on first read)
Five-layer connected-vehicle predictive-maintenance system (sensing → context ingestion → edge processing → cloud sync → service integration) over OBD-II/CAN signals: coolant and intake temperatures, RPM, battery voltage/SoC, tire pressure, brake-wear indicators; validates on an industrial dataset whose failure modes map onto automotive categories (heat dissipation → cooling/thermal, power → battery/electrical, tool wear → pads/tires).

## Relevance to my thesis
The closest published sibling of our Tier-0/1/2 split, and evidence for **what is predictable**: thermal/cooling failure, battery degradation, progressive wear. Confirms our [[thermal drift]] channel is the industry's canonical target and that cloud-side AI is the accepted home for the heavy models.
