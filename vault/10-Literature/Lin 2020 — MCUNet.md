# MCUNet: Tiny Deep Learning on IoT Devices

## Summary (my words to verify on first read)
Co-designs network architecture (TinyNAS) and inference engine (TinyEngine) to fit ImageNet-scale inference into hundreds of KB.

## Relevance to my thesis
Upper-bound proof of what MCU-class silicon can run; its memory-scheduling tricks inform how tight our [[int8]] autoencoder can go.
