# MLPerf Tiny Benchmark

## Summary (my words to verify on first read)
Standard benchmark suite for MCU-class ML — including an anomaly-detection task (autoencoder on machine sounds) with latency/energy metrics.

## Relevance to my thesis
Its anomaly-detection reference model is nearly our DETECT architecture; we reuse its metric methodology for the on-[[S32K3]] evaluation.
