# Graph Neural Network-based Fault Diagnosis: A Review

## Summary (my words to verify on first read)
Reviews fault diagnosis with GNNs: how to build graphs from sensors/systems, which architectures (GCN, GAT, GAE, ST-GCN) fit which fault problems, with experimental comparisons.

## Relevance to my thesis
**The bridge paper** between [[GNN]] and our domain: its graph-construction recipes (sensor-as-node vs component-as-node) directly shape how we encode the K1/K3/bus/service graph.
