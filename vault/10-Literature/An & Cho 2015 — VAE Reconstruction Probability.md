# Variational Autoencoder based Anomaly Detection using Reconstruction Probability

## Summary (my words to verify on first read)
Uses a variational autoencoder's reconstruction *probability* (not raw error) as anomaly score, adding a principled uncertainty flavor.

## Relevance to my thesis
Candidate upgrade of [[reconstruction error]] scoring if the plain [[denoising autoencoder]] threshold proves brittle across operating modes.
