# ToyADMOS: A Dataset of Miniature-Machine Operating Sounds for Anomalous Sound Detection

## Summary (my words to verify on first read)
Anomalous-sound dataset from miniature machines with deliberately damaged components; companion to MIMII in DCASE challenges.

## Relevance to my thesis
Secondary audio benchmark; useful because miniature machines resemble our bench-scale rig acoustics more than factory pumps do.
