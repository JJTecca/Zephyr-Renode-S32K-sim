# MIMII Dataset: Sound Dataset for Malfunctioning Industrial Machine Investigation and Inspection

## Summary (my words to verify on first read)
Public dataset of normal and anomalous sounds of fans, pumps, valves and slide rails recorded with real factory background noise; the standard benchmark for unsupervised anomalous sound detection (DCASE Task 2).

## Relevance to my thesis
**Our microphone channel de-risker.** Before the rig produces audio-feature telemetry, the [[acoustic anomaly detection]] pipeline (FFT bands → autoencoder) is validated here. Anchors [[MIMII]].
